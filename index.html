The SuperKools are a five piece band from northern Indiana entertaining crowds with a mixture of pop, soul, and rock and roll.

Based in Warsaw, Indiana, we’ve played countless shows and gigs across Indiana.

We’re a songwriting powerhouse, constantly creating and gearing up for our next big release!
